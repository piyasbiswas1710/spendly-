Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2, 2026

Spendly is a personal expense tracking service. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We believe in keeping things simple: we only collect what we need to make the service work for you.

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect basic account information such as your name and email address. When you use Spendly, we store the expense records you choose to log — including the amount, category, date, and any description you provide.

We also collect standard technical information sent by your browser, such as your IP address and the type of device you are using, in order to keep the service secure and reliable.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect solely to operate and improve Spendly. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • Display your expense history, category summaries, and time-period views.
  • Persist your records across devices and sessions.
  • Diagnose issues and prevent abuse of the service.

We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in secure databases accessible only to authorised systems. We retain your expense records for as long as your account is active. If you choose to delete your account, your records will be removed from active storage within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law.

You can review, update, or delete individual expenses at any time from within the application.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly relies on a small number of trusted third parties to operate — for example, hosting providers that run the application and database, and email services that send you account-related messages. These providers only receive the information they need to perform their function and are required to handle it securely.

We do not integrate advertising networks or analytics services that track you across other sites.

5. Your Choices

You may access and update your account information at any time. You may also delete individual expenses or close your account entirely. If you have questions about your data or wish to exercise any of these choices, please contact us using the details below.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact details provided in the application. We will do our best to respond promptly.
